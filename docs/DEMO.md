# Slabline demonstration

Use Devnet and sign in. These links open repeatable account-backed rehearsals; they do not trade real Gemini contracts or spend mainnet funds. Wallet **Add $100** replenishes rehearsal cash. Settings **Finish a replay** lets the presenter select a saved rehearsal position and apply Yes/No/Void. Select the matching subject: San Antonio for cards, Omniverse for Jensen.

## Player slab

[Open Wembanyama](https://web-production-ae51051f.up.railway.app/demo?stake=39.90).

Say: **“Back San Antonio, see the Wembanyama slab your position can cover, and claim it if your prediction wins.”**

From an empty open position, $39.90 buys 145 YES shares using recorded public Gemini depth. Review and confirm, then apply Yes wins in Settings. Portfolio → Claim shows the archived $145 Wembanyama PSA 10 card. Select it and finish the existing wallet-controlled Solana Devnet transfer. This produces a devnet representation; it does not acquire the mainnet slab or physical card. The archived card remains selectable even if its live listing changes.

## No slab → Grail

[Open Jensen fallback](https://web-production-ae51051f.up.railway.app/demo?scenario=grail&stake=25).

Say: **“There’s no Jensen Huang slab listed, so this NVIDIA prediction automatically turns a win into JENSEN on Grail.”**

The public Collector Crypt search for Jensen returned zero rows and no next cursor on November 19, 2026. Grail returned the JENSEN token on Robinhood, address `0xa6b0e654bdefc8cff8e9b0c90388768be404d921`, at $0.000239255317 (rounded to 12 decimals). Sources are stored in `DEMO_GRAIL`.

The market identity comes from the archived NVIDIA earnings-call event, outcome Omniverse. Prediction depth is explicitly illustrative at 25¢. $25 buys 100 shares. After applying Yes wins, the full $100 payout automatically becomes a saved simulated JENSEN holding, visible in Portfolio. Duplicate settlement does not buy twice. Reload to demonstrate persistence. A loss creates no tokens; a void refunds remaining position cost.

This is an end-to-end devnet ledger rehearsal. It does not broadcast an EVM swap, acquire real JENSEN, or mint a substitute NFT. Real automatic purchase still requires verified Gemini withdrawal permissions, a funded owned EVM wallet, an executable funding route, and signed/reconciled Grail execution. Do not present it as already wired for mainnet.

## Build and reduce one position

[Open position demo](https://web-production-ae51051f.up.railway.app/demo?stake=25).

Say: **“Add to the same prediction, move up to a bigger card, or sell some shares and keep the cash.”**

Buy once, enter another stake, and buy again. The same open position grows and its entry price is weighted by total cost / shares. The reward preview includes existing shares plus the proposed order, and its target button prices only the additional shares needed. After a buy the stake resets to zero, showing the held position's payout without counting the order twice. Set Shares to sell to a partial amount, review the bid quote, and confirm. Remaining shares keep their proportional cost basis; proceeds credit cash. Rebuy into the remaining position or use Max to exit. Buying after a full exit starts a new position.

A binary winning share pays $1: 100 shares pays $100; adding 100 shares makes the winning payout $200. YES and NO, live-data positions and rehearsals remain separate.

## Cash and sharing

Say: **“Cards are optional. Take the win in cash, or share your position with one link.”**

After a normal rehearsal wins, Portfolio → Claim → Claim cash releases the full payout to available balance. Cash cannot be claimed again or after allocating the same reward to cards. Share opens the existing preview and explicit publication flow; the resulting link and PNG are public snapshots, not live account access. Public profiles remain opt-in.

Cash here is simulated USD credit. Real USDC settlement/withdrawal remains gated; no real token movement is implied by the cash button.
